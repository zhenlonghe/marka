# Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Marka does not collect any data. Your documents stay on your Mac.

## What Marka does not do

  • - No account, sign-up or login.
  • - No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • - No servers of its own. Nothing you write is sent anywhere.

## Your documents

Marka runs in the macOS App Sandbox. It opens only the files and folders you choose, and saves them where they are. Images you paste are saved next to your document.

The editor shows only images stored on your Mac. If a document links to an image on the web, that image may be downloaded when you export the document to PDF or rich text, or preview it with Quick Look, the same way a web browser would load it. The request goes to the website hosting the image, not to the developer.

## Settings

Preferences, such as the editing mode or the folder shown in the sidebar, are stored on your Mac inside Marka's own container. They never leave it.

## Purchases

The 14-day trial and Marka Pro are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. To check whether they are active, Marka asks the App Store on your Mac. The developer does not receive your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Links

A link in a document opens in your web browser only when you Command-click it. What happens after that is between you and that website.

## Quick Look

The Quick Look extension reads only the Markdown file being previewed, to draw the preview. It keeps nothing.

## Children

Marka collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

# 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日

Marka 不收集任何数据。你的文档只留在你的 Mac 上。

## Marka 不做的事

  • - 没有账户，不需要注册或登录。
  • - 没有统计分析、崩溃上报、广告或任何形式的追踪。
  • - 没有自己的服务器。你写的任何内容都不会被发送出去。

## 你的文档

Marka 在 macOS App 沙盒中运行，只打开你选择的文件和文件夹，并保存在原处。粘贴的图片保存在文档旁边。

编辑器只显示存在你 Mac 上的图片。如果文档引用了网络上的图片，在导出 PDF、拷贝为富文本或用 Quick Look 预览时，这些图片可能会像在浏览器里一样被下载。请求发往托管图片的网站，而不是开发者。

## 设置

编辑模式、边栏文件夹等偏好设置保存在你的 Mac 上 Marka 自己的容器里，不会离开这台 Mac。

## 购买

14 天试用和 Marka Pro 都是 App 内购买，完全由 Apple 处理。为了确认它们是否有效，Marka 会向 Mac 上的 App Store 查询。开发者不会获得你的姓名、邮箱、Apple 账户或付款信息。Apple 如何处理购买，见 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)。

## 链接

文档里的链接只有在你按住 Command 点击时才会在浏览器中打开。之后发生的事与该网站有关。

## Quick Look

Quick Look 扩展只读取正在预览的 Markdown 文件，用来绘制预览，不保留任何内容。

## 儿童

Marka 不收集任何人的信息，包括儿童。

## 变更

如果本政策有变化，新版本会发布在此页面，并更新生效日期。
