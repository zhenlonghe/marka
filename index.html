Marka

A fast, native Markdown editor for Mac, made for Chinese and English writing alike.

Free and Pro

Marka is free to download. Every Pro feature can be tried for 14 days; after that you keep the free version, or unlock Pro once and keep it forever. No subscription.

Free

  • Open and read any Markdown file
  • Edit in source mode
  • Find and replace, with regular expressions
  • The marka command line tool
  • Quick Look previews in Finder

Pro

  • Live mode
  • Export to HTML, PDF and rich text
  • Vim mode
  • Focus and typewriter modes
  • Folder sidebar, Quick Open and outline

Questions

How does the trial work?

Choose Start 14-Day Trial when Marka offers it, or from Marka ▸ Marka Pro…. The trial is a free in-app purchase tied to your Apple Account, so it starts once and follows you to your other Macs. When it ends, Marka goes back to the free version. Your documents and settings are not touched.

I bought Marka Pro, but it is locked on this Mac

Open Marka ▸ Marka Pro… and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I install the command line tool?

Choose Marka ▸ Install Command Line Tool… and pick a folder on your PATH, such as /opt/homebrew/bin. Then marka file.md opens a file, marka . opens a folder, and echo text | marka - opens piped text. If the folder belongs to the system (for example /usr/local/bin), Marka shows a one-line command to run in Terminal instead.

Quick Look does not show Markdown previews

Open Marka once after installing it. If the preview still does not appear, check that Marka is turned on in System Settings ▸ General ▸ Login Items & Extensions ▸ Quick Look.

常见问题

试用怎么算？

在 Marka 弹出的面板里，或 Marka ▸ Marka Pro… 中选择开始 14 天试用。试用是一个免费的 App 内购买，记在你的 Apple 账户上，只能开始一次，换一台 Mac 也一样。到期后 Marka 回到免费版，文档和设置都不受影响。

买了 Pro，这台 Mac 上却没解锁

打开 Marka ▸ Marka Pro…，选择恢复购买。请确认 App Store 登录的是购买时用的 Apple 账户。

怎么安装命令行工具？

选择 Marka ▸ 安装命令行工具…，选一个在 PATH 里的文件夹，比如 /opt/homebrew/bin。之后 marka file.md 打开文件，marka . 打开文件夹，echo text | marka - 打开管道输入。如果文件夹属于系统（比如 /usr/local/bin），Marka 会给出一行命令，在终端里执行即可。

Quick Look 不显示 Markdown 预览

安装后先打开一次 Marka。仍然不显示的话，到系统设置 ▸ 通用 ▸ 登录项与扩展 ▸ Quick Look 里确认 Marka 已打开。
